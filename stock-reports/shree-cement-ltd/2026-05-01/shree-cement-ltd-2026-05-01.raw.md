# Raw Analysis — Shree Cement Ltd (2026-05-01)

## Company Info

- **Name:** Shree Cement Limited
- **NSE Symbol:** SHREECEM | **BSE Code:** 500387 | **ISIN:** INE070A01015
- **Exchange:** NSE (primary) / BSE (dual listed)
- **Sector:** Cement / Building Materials (Industrial Goods & Services)
- **Industry sub-segment:** Grey cement, Premium cement (Bangur Marble, Bangur Power, etc.), RMC (ready-mix concrete), and AAC blocks
- **Market Cap Category:** Large Cap (~Rs.90,689 Cr / ~Rs.88,500 Cr range, depending on data point)
- **Index Membership:** Nifty 200, Nifty Midcap 150 (note: dropped out of Nifty 50 historically; verify current index inclusion). F&O eligible.
- **Headquarters:** Kolkata (Registered Office) / Beawar, Rajasthan (Plant HQ origin)
- **Promoter Group:** Bangur Family (Hari Mohan Bangur, Prashant Bangur)

**Business Description:**
Shree Cement is India's 3rd largest cement producer (after UltraTech and Adani-controlled Ambuja-ACC). Installed grey cement capacity ~46.4 MTPA (Mar 2026 baseline) targeting 72 MTPA by Mar 2026 and 80 MTPA by FY28/FY29. The company is best known for industry-leading cost leadership (lowest production cost among large Indian cement companies — Rs.3,200–3,400 per tonne per Univest), captive power including 634 MW renewable (~61% of power needs), industry-leading fuel cost (Rs.1.56/kcal), and a lean balance sheet (effectively net cash, ~Rs.6,000 Cr cash reserves per Print/Business Standard concall coverage). Strong franchise in North India (25–30% share in Rajasthan, Haryana, UP, Bihar) with growing presence in East and South. Ancillary verticals include AAC blocks, RMC (ready-mix concrete — expanding from 19 to 45 plants), and building materials.

## Fundamental Analysis

### A. Business Quality

**Moat:**
- **Cost Leadership** (durable): Lowest grey-cement production cost among Indian large players (Rs.3,200–3,400/t per Univest); fuel cost Rs.1.56/kcal (industry-leading); 634 MW captive renewable (~61% of power) — provides structural margin advantage even when realizations soften.
- **Operational Excellence:** Best-in-sector EBITDA/t (Rs.1,521 in Q1 FY26) vs UltraTech ~Rs.917 in Q3 FY26 — a Rs.500–600 spread per tonne.
- **Regional Brand Strength:** 25–30% market share in Rajasthan, Haryana, UP, Bihar (Bangur brand premium).
- **Premiumisation Lever:** Premium products = 22% of trade volumes in Q3 FY26 (vs 15% in Q2 FY26) — supports realisation in a soft pricing market.
- **Capital Discipline:** Net-cash balance sheet enables counter-cyclical capacity additions and aggressive RMC buildout while peers lever up.

**Management:**
- Promoter-led (Bangur family — H.M. Bangur, Prashant Bangur). Long track record of conservative growth and capital efficiency.
- MD Neeraj Akhoury — ex-Ambuja CEO, deep cement industry credentials. Recent commentary cautiously optimistic on infra/construction demand drivers.
- Strategic clarity: explicitly avoiding M&A (vs. UltraTech/Adani consolidation play), preferring organic capacity expansion to 80 MTPA.

**Corporate Governance:**
- 0% promoter pledge (clean).
- Standard SEBI compliance — Q4 FY26 dematerialisation confirmation filed Apr 7, 2026 (per Business Upturn).
- Outstanding GST demand of Rs.11.40 Cr (Belagavi, Karnataka) — being contested; immaterial vs. balance sheet.
- No history of accounting controversies.

**Score (Business Quality): 8/10**

## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case (3–5 reasons)

1. **Best-in-sector cost structure intact** — Rs.1,521 EBITDA/t in Q1 FY26 vs UltraTech ~Rs.917 in Q3 FY26. 634 MW renewable + Rs.1.56/kcal fuel cost provides ~Rs.500–600/t structural margin advantage that survives any pricing cycle.
2. **Capacity ramp 46.4 → 72 → 80 MTPA** delivers ~70% volume growth vs FY25 by FY28/29, anchoring an EPS doubling story over a 3–4 year horizon.
3. **Net-cash balance sheet (~Rs.6,000 Cr cash, debt-free)** funds Meghalaya Rs.1,800 Cr + RMC + Rs.500 Cr base capex with minimal financial risk and protects through any sector down-cycle.
4. **Q4 FY26 earnings inflection** — analyst consensus Rs.5,800–6,400 Cr revenue / Rs.800–1,000 Cr PAT (+39–74% YoY) sets up FY27 EPS upgrade momentum.
5. **Premiumisation (22% of trade in Q3) + RMC scale-up (19 → 45 plants)** lifts mix-led realisation independent of headline cement prices.
6. **DII / mutual fund accumulation** (DII +120 bps QoQ; SBI MF 5.34%, ICICI Pru 3.37%) provides domestic liquidity support amid FII outflows.

### Bear Case (3–5 reasons)

1. **Valuation premium hard to defend** — 48–68x P/E vs UltraTech 49x, Ambuja 22.7x, ACC 9.4x. Any FY27 guidance miss or margin slip risks 25–30% de-rating.
2. **Sector oversupply** — 158 MT capacity additions by FY28; pricing CAGR negative 1–2% FY23–26; capacity utilisation stuck at 67–68% — pricing power likely capped.
3. **North India region (Shree's stronghold) seeing fastest expansion at 11% CAGR** vs sector 7.4% — direct margin pressure in core franchise.
4. **Cost inflation in petcoke (50–60% imported), freight, polypropylene packaging** driven by West Asia tensions — limited ability to pass through.
5. **Meghalaya plant faces environmental clearance challenge** — MP has formally raised cumulative-impact concerns; potential 6–18 month delay would reset the 80 MTPA narrative.
6. **FII exit accelerating** (8.94% from 10.07%; 583 → 527 FPIs) on US tariff worries — overhang on stock.

### Base Case

Stock will likely trade in a Rs.24,000–30,000 band over the next 6–9 months. Q4 FY26 results (reportedly Apr 28, 2026 board meeting) is the proximate catalyst — a beat ≥ Rs.900 Cr PAT + dividend ≥ Rs.120/share + constructive FY27 commentary on volume / EBITDA/t / 80 MTPA timeline pushes price toward Rs.28,000–30,000 (in line with consensus Rs.27,727–28,602). A miss or muted commentary risks a slip to Rs.22,000–24,000. Over a 12–18 month horizon, the consensus Rs.28,602 target implies ~13–15% upside from CMP — moderate-positive risk-reward, but only attractive on dips toward Rs.22,000–24,000 given the valuation premium and sector oversupply overhang. **House view:** Hold / Accumulate on dips; not a fresh-money compounder at current price.
