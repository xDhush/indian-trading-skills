## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case

1. **Best-in-sector cost structure:** Q1 FY26 EBITDA per tonne of **Rs. 1,521** vs UltraTech's ~Rs. 917 in Q3 FY26 — a Rs. 500–600/tonne structural advantage that survives even soft pricing cycles.
2. **Capacity ramp delivers ~70% volume growth:** 46.4 → 72 → 80 MTPA over FY26–FY29 anchors an EPS-doubling story over 3–4 years.
3. **Net-cash balance sheet:** ~Rs. 6,000 Cr cash, effectively debt-free. Funds the Rs. 1,800 Cr Meghalaya plant + RMC build + Rs. 500 Cr base capex with minimal financial risk.
4. **Q4 FY26 earnings inflection:** Consensus expects revenue Rs. 5,800–6,400 Cr and PAT Rs. 800–1,000 Cr (+39–74% YoY vs Q4 FY25's Rs. 574 Cr).
5. **Premiumisation + RMC scale-up:** Premium products rose from 15% to 22% of trade volumes (Q2 → Q3 FY26); RMC plants scaling 19 → 45 in 6–8 months with ~45% captive cement absorption.
6. **DII / mutual fund accumulation:** DII holding +120 bps QoQ; SBI MF (5.34%) and ICICI Pru (3.37%) leading the way — domestic support amid FII outflows.

### Bear Case

1. **Premium valuation hard to defend:** P/E of 48–68x vs Ambuja at 22.7x and ACC at 9.4x. Any FY27 guidance miss or margin slip risks 25–30% de-rating.
2. **Sector oversupply:** 158 MT capacity additions by FY28 (7.4% sector CAGR); cement pricing CAGR projected negative 1–2% FY23–FY26.
3. **North India — Shree's stronghold — adding 11% CAGR capacity**, the highest of any region. Direct margin pressure in the core franchise.
4. **Cost inflation:** Petcoke (50–60% imported), freight, polypropylene packaging all rising on West Asia tensions. Pass-through is limited.
5. **Meghalaya plant clearance risk:** An MP has formally raised cumulative environmental impact concerns. A 6–18 month delay would reset the 80 MTPA narrative.
6. **FII exit accelerating:** Holding fell from 10.07% to 8.94% in one quarter (FPI count 583 → 527) on US tariff uncertainty.

### Base Case

The stock likely trades in a **Rs. 24,000 – 30,000** band over the next 6–9 months. Q4 FY26 results (board meeting Apr 28, 2026) is the proximate catalyst — a beat above Rs. 900 Cr PAT, dividend ≥ Rs. 120/share, and a constructive FY27 commentary push the price toward Rs. 28,000–30,000. A miss or muted commentary risks a slip toward Rs. 22,000–24,000. Over 12–18 months, consensus implies ~13–15% upside — moderate, not exceptional, given valuation and sector overhangs.

- **Sector demand normalisation to 70–71% utilisation** per ICRA — sustained pricing power return.

## Conclusion

Shree Cement is a high-quality cement franchise — lowest production cost in the industry, debt-free balance sheet, and a clear runway from 46.4 to 80 MTPA by FY28/29. The earnings cycle is bottoming with a sharp Q4 FY26 inflection priced in by analysts. But the stock already trades at a steep premium (P/E 48–68x) that demands flawless delivery on FY27 guidance, while sector oversupply and FII outflows cap upside. **Hold for existing investors; accumulate on dips toward Rs. 22,000–24,000 for new positions.**

**What to watch:**

- **Q4 FY26 PAT (Apr 28 result)** — Expected Rs. 800–1,000 Cr. Watch for: beat above Rs. 1,000 Cr (bullish) vs miss below Rs. 800 Cr (bearish).
- **EBITDA per tonne trajectory** — Q3 FY26 was Rs. 1,040; Q1 FY26 was Rs. 1,521. Watch for: sustained recovery toward Rs. 1,200+ in Q4 / FY27.
- **Capacity utilisation** — Currently 67–68% sector-wide. Watch for: move toward 70–71% in FY27 (pricing power signal).
- **FII holding** — Currently 8.94%, down from 10.07% QoQ. Watch for: stabilisation or further declines below 8%.
- **Meghalaya clearance status** — Watch for: resolution of MP-raised environmental concerns; any 6+ month delay resets the 80 MTPA narrative.
- **Cement price trend** — Pan-India ~Rs. 340/bag, +1.5–2% MoM. Watch for: holding above Rs. 340 through summer construction season.

**Review triggers:**

- A Q4 FY26 PAT miss below Rs. 800 Cr or FY27 EBITDA per tonne guidance below Rs. 1,000 → downgrade to Reduce.
- Stock breaks below Rs. 22,000 with adverse fundamental news → re-evaluate support and consider step-in buying.
- Meghalaya clearance suspended for >12 months → reset capacity assumption to 72 MTPA and lower target.

**Bottom line:** A quality compounder at a full price — best accumulated on dips, not chased at current levels.
