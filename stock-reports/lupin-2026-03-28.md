## Investment Thesis

### Bull Case

1. US business momentum is exceptional — Rs. 3,113 Cr in Q3 FY26 (+54% YoY), now contributing 44% of revenue. Pipeline of complex generics (inhalation, injectables) provides pricing power.
2. Margin trajectory is the most compelling aspect: EBITDA margin expanded 840 bps YoY to 33.5% in Q3 FY26. Operating leverage is real.
3. Net profit CAGR of ~55% over 5 years; PAT grew 73% TTM. Earnings quality is improving.
4. No promoter pledge and stable ~47% promoter holding signals governance confidence.
5. Pitolisant (narcolepsy) received tentative US FDA approval March 25, 2026 — CNS portfolio expansion with limited competition.

### Bear Case

1. Current price (Rs. 2,349) exceeds the median analyst target (Rs. 2,287–2,338) — limited near-term upside priced in.
2. Ankleshwar facility received a Form 483 with two FDA observations in March 2026 — minor but a regulatory overhang.
3. UBS downgraded Lupin alongside Dr. Reddy's and Zydus citing GLP-1 market competition concerns for Indian pharma US generics revenue.
4. Long-term EPS growth forecast by some analysts is just ~0.8% annually — concern about growth sustainability.
5. Free cash flow turned negative in FY25 (Rs. -1,172 Cr) due to elevated capex — watch for FY26 normalization.

### Base Case

Lupin delivers Rs. 24,000–25,000 Cr revenue in FY26 with ~22–24% operating margins. EPS approaches Rs. 95–100. Stock fair value is Rs. 2,200–2,400 range at 23–25x earnings.

## Conclusion

Lupin is a fundamentally improved company compared to 2–3 years ago. The margin turnaround is real and the US business is outperforming. However, at Rs. 2,349 — essentially at the 52-week high and above the average analyst target — the near-term risk/reward is balanced, not compelling.

**Key monitoring parameters:**

- EBITDA margin sustaining above 25% (current 33.5% has high-bar comps ahead)
- Q4 FY26 US sales trajectory
- Ankleshwar FDA resolution timeline
- FCF turning positive in FY26

**Suggested review triggers:** Q4 FY26 results, any new FDA warning letters, or stock price below Rs. 2,150 (Support 2 — better risk/reward entry).
